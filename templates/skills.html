The voice

The system prompt, the provider, the model, and the output cap — everything that decides how the grade is written.

The standards

The rubric the skill grades against, criterion by criterion, with the points each one carries.

The materials

Every knowledge document attached to the skill — syllabi, rubric conventions, worked examples — sent natively, no OCR.

No student names, no submissions, and no API keys are ever written into a bundle. A colleague who imports one grades the way you do without seeing anyone in your class.
